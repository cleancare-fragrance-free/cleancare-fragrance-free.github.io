You pick up a bottle marked “unscented.” Does that mean it contains no fragrance? Not necessarily. The language on a label is a starting point, but it does not tell the whole story.

## Two terms worth separating

The US Environmental Protection Agency explains that an **unscented** product may contain ingredients used to mask the smell of other ingredients. Its fragrance-free guidance distinguishes products made without fragrance materials or masking scents. Read the [EPA’s fragrance-free fact sheet](https://www.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2016-10/documents/saferchoice-factsheet-fragrancefree_0.pdf) for that distinction.

## What a verified label can add

The EPA offers an additional **Fragrance-Free** designation for qualifying Safer Choice products. It involves review to confirm that the product contains no fragrance materials. The standard Safer Choice label alone does not mean fragrance-free. See the [EPA’s criteria for fragrance-free products](https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice/safer-choice-criteria-fragrance-free-products).

This is a specific US certification program, not a universal definition covering every product or country. A label on one product should not be assumed to apply to every item from the same brand.

## A practical way to compare

1. **Read the exact product name.** Similar packaging can cover several differently scented versions.
2. **Check the current ingredient information.** Look on the packaging and the manufacturer’s website for the exact regional formula.
3. **Ask when something is unclear.** A useful question is: “Does this version contain fragrance materials, including masking fragrance?”
4. **Keep your own notes.** Save the product name, region, and date you checked so you can revisit the information later.

## Start with one everyday choice

Choose one product you already use and learn a little more about it. You do not need to replace your whole routine at once. Clearer information is a useful first step; a fragrance-free claim is not a promise that a product will suit every person.
