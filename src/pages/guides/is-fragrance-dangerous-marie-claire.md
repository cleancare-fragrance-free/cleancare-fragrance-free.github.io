## Read the original

[Marie Claire’s article, by beauty journalist Lottie Winter](https://www.marieclaire.co.uk/beauty/fragrance/is-fragrance-dangerous), responds to Miranda Kerr’s discussion with Dr. Mark Hyman. It questions broad claims about fragrance toxicity while discussing irritation and allergy. This is journalism, not an article authored by a physician.

## Keep the questions separate

Someone’s decision to avoid perfume is not the same as evidence that every fragrance causes the same harm. The article highlights why ingredients, dose and the type of research matter when interpreting claims.

For CleanCare readers, the useful distinction is between reducing an exposure that triggers personal symptoms and asserting a universal disease risk. A fragrance-free routine can be a practical choice without making the latter claim.
