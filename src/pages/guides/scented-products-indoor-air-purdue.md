## A house built to measure invisible chemistry

Purdue engineers Nusrat Jung and Brandon Boor use a highly instrumented “tiny house” laboratory to study how ordinary activities affect indoor air. Their [February 2025 research report](https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/2025/Q1/air-inside-your-home-may-be-more-polluted-than-outside-due-to-everyday-chemical-products/) describes studies of scented wax melts, cleaners, air fresheners, hair products, and other volatile chemical products.

## What they measured

Fragrance ingredients such as terpenes can react with ozone that enters a building. Under the tested conditions, that chemistry rapidly formed large numbers of nanoscale particles. In one study, scented wax melts produced particle formation despite having no flame.

The linked Purdue page connects readers to the underlying papers, including [“Flame-free candles are not pollution-free”](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.estlett.4c00986) and research on [nanocluster aerosol during use of scented volatile products](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsestair.4c00118).

## What the studies do not yet answer

These studies measure emissions, chemistry, particle formation, and potential respiratory deposition. They do not establish a clinical diagnosis or quantify the long-term health effect of every product in every home. Purdue explicitly notes that toxicology research is still needed to determine the health implications of inhaling these complex mixtures.

Product formula, amount used, ventilation, ozone levels, room size, and frequency all influence exposure. The useful conclusion is therefore source-aware: heavily scented products can actively alter indoor chemistry, and reducing optional sources is a practical precaution while health research continues.

## Practical response

1. Remove or pause products intended only to add scent, including wax melts and air fresheners.
2. Ventilate during cleaning and hair-product use when outdoor air conditions allow.
3. Choose unsprayed or fragrance-free options where practical.
4. Follow safety directions and never mix cleaning chemicals.
