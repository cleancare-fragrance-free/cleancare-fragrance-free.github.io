# Brand identification artwork

Official wordmarks and compact site marks are displayed only to identify catalogue brands. Trademarks and image rights remain with their respective owners; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

The asset URLs and filenames are recorded in `src/data/brand-logos.mjs`, observed 6 October 2026 on the official pages referenced by `src/data/product-catalog.mjs`. ANESSA and VERDIO use their dedicated official brand sites. La Roche-Posay's logo is the image displayed in its official website's cookie notice. Some brands use their official site mark when the header wordmark is inline rather than an image file.

Assets are served locally, without a third-party logo lookup service. SEEN, Clinique and Vaseline retain text fallbacks until their official logo files can be retrieved. The SEEN site displayed a certificate warning, which was not bypassed. Recheck artwork and permission requirements before wider promotional reuse.
